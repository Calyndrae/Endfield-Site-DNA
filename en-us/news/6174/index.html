[Military Grade Issue] LTO Details

Dear Endministrator,

While [Military Grade Issue] is available, the chance of getting 6★ weapon [Type 42: Solemn Phalanx (Arts Unit)] is increased!


▼// [Military Grade Issue]

· Availability: Opens after [Homecoming] version update; ends after 3 [Chartered Headhunting] banners (starting from [North Yearns the Rift Vigile])

· Participation Criteria: Complete the main mission [Chapter I Process I: Break the Siege]

· [Military Grade Issue] features the Rate-UP 6★ weapon [Type 42: Solemn Phalanx (Arts Unit)].

· 6★ weapons that may appear in [Military Grade Issue] include: Type 42: Solemn Phalanx (Arts Unit) / Former Finery (Greatsword) / Wedge (Handcannon) / Eminent Repute (Sword) / JET (Polearm) / Detonation Unit (Arts Unit) / Oblivion (Arts Unit).


▼// [Arsenal Issue] Rewards

In every [Arsenal Issue], performing a set number of issues gives the banner's specific [Arms Offering Crate] (Arms OC) or [Rate-UP 6★ Weapon] in alternating order. The total number of issues for these Arsenal Issue rewards will be reset once the banner ends, and will not carry over to other [Arsenal Issue] banners.


▼// [Arsenal Issue] Details

· In [Arsenal Issue], you can spend [Arsenal Tickets] to apply for an issue.

· Each issue gives 10 random weapons from the list of available weapons, including at least one 5★ or better weapon.


▼// [Arsenal Exchange] Details

In [Arsenal Exchange], the [Arsenal Ticket] can also be used to directly purchase weapons. Some purchasable weapons may be updated every now and then. Check the information displayed on the [Acquisition Center - Arsenal Exchange] screen for what is actually available.


※ For more details and reward rules, visit the in-game [Acquisition Center - Arsenal Exchange].
