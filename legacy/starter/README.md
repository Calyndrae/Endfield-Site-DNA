# Neutral child-site starter

Open [index.html](index.html) locally. It demonstrates the handbook's measured family traits with original abstract art and content: scaled root rem, fixed rail and portrait menu, white editorial stage, yellow chapter band, six/three-column card system, two working filters, detail dialog, real-task loader, staged reveals, hover and keyboard focus, and a dark centered footer.

Change the content in `site.js` and replace the CSS-generated abstract art with your own portrait and hero assets. Add actual policy/contact links and localization before publishing. The remote WOFF2 declarations are references to the official site's declared font files; local fallbacks remain functional when offline. The technical rules and original-source evidence are in [the single-page handbook](../handbook/index.html), with 29 anchored sections.
